JAADU SNAP - snap your fingers on camera and turn to dust
Open source (MIT): https://github.com/AyanAhmedKhan/snapp-filter-gmeet
Based on Jaadu Snap by Nidhi Dadiya: https://github.com/dadiyanidhi/jaadu-snap

INSTALL (2 minutes)
1. Unzip jaadu-snap.zip into a plain folder that you won't delete, for example C:\Extensions\
   Avoid OneDrive / Dropbox folders (sync can break the extension).
2. Open Chrome and go to chrome://extensions
3. Turn on "Developer mode" (top right).
4. Click "Load unpacked" and pick the jaadu-snap folder (the one with manifest.json inside).
5. Pin it from the puzzle-piece icon, then reload any open meeting tabs.

USE IT
1. Join Google Meet / Teams (web) / Zoom (web, "Join from your browser") with your camera on.
2. Keep the camera still ~10-20 seconds. The popup says "Ready" when it has learned your room.
3. Raise your hand to face or chest height, palm to the camera, press thumb and middle
   finger together, then snap. You turn to dust.
4. Snap again to come back.
   Keyboard backup: Alt+Shift+1 (vanish / come back).

TIPS
- Best empty room: click "Capture empty room" in the popup and step out of frame for ~5 seconds.
  Otherwise the spot right behind you is filled in by AI and can look soft/blurry.
- Moved the camera? Click "Relearn room".
- A single snap waits ~0.7 s in case it's a double snap (double snap = cash rain).
  Untick "Snap twice -> cash rain" in the popup to make single snaps instant.
- Auto-mute: your mic goes silent while you're vanished and comes back when you snap back.
  The meeting's own mic button won't change (others just hear silence), so check the
  popup: it says "You're vanished. Mic muted" while it's on. If you had muted yourself
  before vanishing, you stay muted after you return. Turn it off with
  "Mute my mic while I'm vanished" in the popup.
- Test it alone first: start an instant meeting on meet.google.com and snap at your self view.
